HoneyBloom Ice Cream

Honey, hibiscus & marigold

HoneyBloom is creamy ice cream sweetened with natural honey and flavored with hibiscus and marigold. Hibiscus adds a gentle floral tang and a natural pink-red color, while marigold brings a soft floral note. It is made in small batches in Western Bicutan, Taguig.

Proposed price ₱70.00 per 8 oz · subject to owner confirmation
✦

What's inside

Creamsada, Nestlé cream, cream cheese, natural honey, hibiscus calyces, marigold petals, and vanilla.

❄

Keep it happy

Keep frozen at -18 °C (0 °F) or colder. Best eaten within 14 days. Do not refreeze melted ice cream.

Our story

A little flower magic in every scoop.

HoneyBloom Ice Cream was imagined for people who love discovering something a little different.

By bringing together honey, hibiscus, and marigold, we create a distinctive floral ice cream experience with a creamy texture and a naturally inspired color. Made in small batches, HoneyBloom is our little way of bringing curiosity and sweetness to the neighborhood.

Our vision

To become a recognized local producer of flower-and-honey-based ice cream in Taguig City, known for distinctive, naturally flavored frozen desserts.

What guides us

Made with purpose.
Shared with joy.

Our mission is to create flavorful, naturally sweetened ice cream using honey and edible flowers such as hibiscus and marigold, while supporting local suppliers and offering a lower-calorie alternative. We are committed to consistent quality, honest product claims, affordable pricing and good service.
